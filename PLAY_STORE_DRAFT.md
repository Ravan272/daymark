# Play Store listing draft

## App name
Daymark — Student Planner

## Short description
Keep track of student tasks and class attendance in one simple offline app.

## Full description
Make your study days easier with Daymark, a simple student planner designed to help you stay organised.

**Tasks**
- Add tasks with an optional subject and due-date label.
- Mark work complete as you finish it.
- Delete tasks or clear completed items.

**Attendance**
- Track attendance for each subject.
- Record classes as Present or Absent.
- View subject-wise and overall attendance percentages.
- Undo the latest attendance entry when needed.

**Private by design**
- Works offline.
- No account required.
- Your entries are saved locally on your device.
- The app does not request internet access.

Daymark is a personal tracking tool. Attendance percentages are based only on classes you enter and may not match your institution's official records.

## Suggested category
Education or Productivity (choose the most appropriate category in Play Console).

## Privacy note
The current source code does not send task or attendance data to a server and does not request internet access. Re-check the final build and all future SDKs/changes before completing Play Console's Data safety form.
